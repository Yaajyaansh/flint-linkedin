I ask every candidate one question before hiring them:

"When was the last time you identified an opportunity for your business and solved it end to end?"

Not assigned to you. Not part of your roadmap. You saw something, decided it mattered, and carried it from idea to done.

The strongest candidates light up at this question. They have three stories and struggle to pick one. The ones I pass on usually describe a project someone handed them.

What I'm testing for is agency. Did you wait for permission, or did you go?

It matters more now than ever. We're a 10-person AI-native team at Flint, and the execution half of most jobs keeps getting cheaper. Agents can build the thing. What they can't do is notice the thing should exist. That's the part I hire for.

It's also why everyone on this team has been a founder or founding engineer before. They all had an answer ready.

(And yes, we're hiring. If you just thought of your story, my DMs are open.)
