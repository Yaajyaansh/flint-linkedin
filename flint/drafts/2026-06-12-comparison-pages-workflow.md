Here's the workflow:

1/ Research: Ask Claude to pull your top competitors with their key differentiators, strengths, and weaknesses vs you.

2/ Keywords: Claude turns that into a "vs" keyword table with estimated search volume, CPC, and competition score for each. Gives you directional data on which pages to build first.

3/ Build: Claude uses the Flint connector to create each page directly on your site. Hero headline, side-by-side comparison table, "when to choose" sections for both products, CTA. Matches your existing design.

4/ Publish: Review in Flint's editor, tweak, publish.
