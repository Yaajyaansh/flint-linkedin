I chatted with two enterprise CMOs recently: @Vanta and @Amplitude. Both of them are looking to solve the same problem:

Nobody at their company agrees on what "on-brand" means. In other words: brand governance.

SEO, ABM, product marketing, and engineering are all building pages independently… and nobody knows what the others made.

Scott at Vanta had a clear vision for what the solution should look like: a brand store.

> Central repository of approved assets
> Reusable page and campaign blocks
> HQ defines the system once, and regional teams add the last 10-20%.

What he liked about Flint was that this model already exists.

The work is already 80% done, teams get what they need without starting from scratch every time.

You can piece this together with Claude + Vercel. But page generation is the easy part.

Brand consistency across multiple teams, shared templates, QA before publish, etc… is the infrastructure that actually requires a lot of work to build.
