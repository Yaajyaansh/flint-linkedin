LinkedIn Two coaches gave me opposite advice this week on our first sales hire. One said: don't hire an AE yet. You don't have a repeatable motion for 5-figure deals. Hire a junior SDR instead. The other said: hire a senior person who will do ground-level work for 6-9 months without resisting it. The SDR vs. AE debate at an early stage startup is the wrong question. The right question is: will this person make cold calls, write sequences, and show up to demos without needing the title to justify the work? I've seen AEs who won't prospect. I've seen SDRs who close. The title tells you nothing. The question to ask in the interview: &quot;What's the most unglamorous thing you did in your last role, and how long did you do it before it changed?&quot; If they can't answer that, don't hire them.
