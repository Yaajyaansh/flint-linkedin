The idea is simple. You already personalize the email. But then it links to your homepage, the same page everyone sees. A prospect in healthcare should land on a page with healthcare case studies, their pain points, their use case.

Here's how it works:

1/ Drop in your list of target companies into Clay.

2/ Clay researches each one: company size, who to target, contact info, relevant use cases.

3/ That research gets passed to Flint, which generates a personalized landing page for each prospect based on their industry and pain points.

4/ The page URL drops into the outbound email automatically.

5/ Hit send. Every prospect gets a page built specifically for them.

Setup takes under an hour. The same workflow works for event follow-ups and gifting campaigns too.
