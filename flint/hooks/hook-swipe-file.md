# Hook Swipe File — Adapted for Michelle / Flint

External hooks from high-performing founders. Use the Template column to adapt for Michelle's context. Do NOT copy the original hook — it's sourced from a different founder's voice and industry. Use it to identify the structural pattern, then apply to Michelle's real experience.

Source: ~198 founder hooks from Eli Rubel, Peep Laja, Chris Walker, Adam Robinson, Jason Lemkin, Millie H., Tyler Leber, and others.

---

## Milestone + Near-Miss / Reversal

**Original:** "I just signed our first 4 clients and booked $14,000 MRR for Profit Labs. But two weeks ago, I almost shut the whole business down. Here's what changed:"

**Template:** I just [achieved positive milestone] and [impressive metric]. But [short time period] ago, I almost [gave up/pivoted/shut down]. Here's what changed:

**For Michelle:** Any Flint inflection point — a month Flint almost ran out of runway, a hire that almost didn't happen, a product decision that nearly went wrong.

---

## Time Contrast (then vs. now)

**Original:** "4 years ago, we charged $45,000/mo at Matter Made. Today, our retainer fee starts at $8,000/mo. It flies in the face of everything I've ever said about 'raising your prices,' but it was the single best decision we could've made."

**Template:** [Time period] ago, [past state]. Today, [current state]. It flies in the face of [common wisdom], but it was the single best decision we could've made.

**For Michelle:** Pricing changes at Flint, positioning shifts, strategy reversals. Works well for "we used to believe X, now we believe Y."

---

## Volume + Output = Insight

**Original:** "Last month, I extracted 15 LinkedIn posts from one 60-minute conversation. In 5 minutes, I saw exactly why most agencies struggle with content."

**Template:** Last [time period], I [accomplished impressive output] from one [input]. In [short time], I saw exactly why most [audience] struggle with [challenge].

**For Michelle:** Customer visits that generated product insights, calls that surfaced a pattern, a demo that changed how Michelle pitches.

---

## Contrarian Desire

**Original:** "I have no desire to get my startup to $100,000,000 ARR. Because I know what that takes, and I'm not willing to give it."

**Template:** I have no desire to [achieve seemingly desirable goal]. Because I know what that takes, and I'm not willing to give it.

**For Michelle:** Flint's deliberate choices to not do something most companies do — no enterprise sales motion, no growth-at-all-costs, no hiring from big tech for its own sake.

---

## Measurement Error = Missed Goal

**Original:** "Measuring 'Inbound' vs. 'Outbound' is probably the #1 reason you're missing your Growth targets."

**Template:** Measuring "[common metric A]" vs. "[common metric B]" is probably the #1 reason you're missing [important goal].

**For Michelle:** Marketing measurement traps, attribution errors, vanity metrics that hide real pipeline problems. Very on-brand for Flint's ICP.

## Never-Done-Before + Result

**Original:** "I've never done this before. I'm revealing the schedule that let me generate $9.8M in profit since 2020 while working 25h/week:"

**Template:** I've never done this before. I'm revealing the [system/process] that let me [achieve impressive result] while [constraint]:

**For Michelle:** Could work for behind-the-scenes Flint playbooks — the outbound system that closed $X, the content calendar that drove pipeline, the hiring process.

## Co-Founder Pair: Past → Present

**Original:** "5 years ago, my co-founder Julian Wimbush and I would walk past a 800 sqft office everyday that seemed like a complete pipedream. Today, we have 25+ employees in a space 5x as nice."

**Template:** [Time period] ago, my [partner] and I would [routine] past [small dream] that seemed like a complete pipedream. Today, [impressive current state].

**For Michelle:** Michelle and Max's early days — working from coffee shops, the first Jackson Square office, the team's growth. Works especially well with a before/after photo.

## Volume → Pattern → Lesson

**Original:** "I've sat through 500+ forecast calls. Nothing kills team morale and pipeline clarity faster than a rep telling a 10-minute story that ends with 'we're waiting on next steps.'"

**Template:** I've [experienced] [large number]+ [activities]. Nothing kills [outcome] faster than [common bad practice].

**For Michelle:** 20+ sales calls a week, 15 customer visits in 3 days, 50+ product demos. Michelle has density of experience that earns this structure.

## Surprising Downshift

**Original:** "I just got back from a 3-week Europe trip with my family. I barely worked, yet revenue continued to grow. Here's the 6-year journey that made that possible."

**Template:** I just [desirable activity]. I barely [worked], yet [business metric] continued to [grow]. Here's the [N-year] journey that made that possible.

**For Michelle:** Would work if/when Flint reaches a point where the systems run without Michelle's constant input. Not ready yet — don't use until it's earned.

## Friend's Failure as Warning

**Original:** "A CEO friend of mine just shut down his startup after 12 years. He raised over $30m in VC... I was shocked at what he said when I turned him down."

**Template:** A [title] friend of mine just [negative outcome] after [time period]. They [impressive credential], and [additional context]. I was shocked at what they said when [pivotal moment].

**For Michelle:** Used sparingly. Works when there's a real person, real stakes, and a specific insight that only Michelle could draw from proximity to this story.

## Common Practice → Handcuffs

**Original:** "Stock options are more like handcuffs than an incentive."

**Template:** [Common business practice] are more like [negative metaphor] than [intended purpose].

**For Michelle:** Contrarian takes on marketing tech, ad spend, comparison pages, CMS systems. Short and punchy — works well on X.

## Specific-Number Stack

**Original:** "144 LinkedIn posts. $5,100 in ad spend. 138 booked meetings. Here's the step-by-step workflow most agencies will never show you."

**Template:** [Number] [content type]. [Dollar amount] in [investment]. [Number] [desired outcome]. Here's the step-by-step workflow most [competitors] will never show you.

**For Michelle:** Any time Flint has a concrete input → output ratio to share. Graphite's conversion doubling. BoomPop's 0→10 conversions. Pages-per-minute benchmarks.

## Waste → Stack That Worked

**Original:** "I wasted $150k testing 100+ AI sales tools so you don't have to. Here's the exact 25-tool stack that took us to $6M ARR."

**Template:** I wasted [dollar amount] testing [number]+ [tools/solutions] so you don't have to. Here's the exact [number]-[type] stack that took us to [result].

**For Michelle:** Marketing stack at Flint, SEO experiments, paid acquisition tests, AI tools for growth. Works whenever there's a real story of trial and error with a payoff.

## Trait Counter-Expectation

**Original:** "The most successful 'founder' I know never raised a dollar of VC funding, never had a LinkedIn profile, and started her first 'business' at age 8."

**Template:** The most successful [role] I know never [common expectation 1], never [common expectation 2], and [surprising characteristic].

**For Michelle:** Could work for a mentor post about someone who defied the typical founder profile.

## Percent + Fatal Pattern

**Original:** "I've audited 200+ founder profiles this year. 73% made the same fatal mistake."

**Template:** I've audited [number]+ [target] [area] this year. [Percentage]% made the same fatal mistake.

**For Michelle:** ABM lead audits, landing page audits, marketing site reviews. Michelle has done dozens of these. Works if she has real data behind the number.

## Single Obvious Statement

**Original:** "This cold email will never stop working."
**Original:** "The AI SDR is dying."
**Original:** "The WORST way to do strategy"

**For Michelle:** Best as X tweets. Start with a declarative that takes a real position. No qualifier, no hedge. Works for Flint's product opinions: "The marketing site is now a product surface." / "The CMS is dead." / "AEO will beat SEO."

## Authority + Surprising Action

**Original:** "One of the most important lessons on leadership that I ever learned was from hearing Kevin Huang, 9th employee at DoorDash, share how he personally assembled tables so that employees could get their work done."

**Template:** One of the most important lessons on [topic] I ever learned was from [specific person], [their credential], share how [specific humble/unexpected action].

**For Michelle:** Works for mentor stories — Stewart Butterfield, Jeff Wilke, Sheryl Sandberg. Lead with the specific action they took, not the relationship or the lesson.

## Cultural Moment Reaction

**Original:** "Everyone's freaking out about decreased LinkedIn reach, but no one's doing anything about it. Here are 7 switches you can make today."

**Template:** Everyone's freaking out about [current problem], but no one's doing anything about it. Here are [number] switches you can make today.

**For Michelle:** AI replacing marketing jobs, Google algorithm changes, Claude API pricing, any moment her audience is anxious about something. Works well on the day it drops.
