# top_posts.md — Michelle Lim / Flint

## How to use this doc

These are Michelle's real published posts, ranked by engagement. Read them before drafting. They show what her voice looks like at the sentence level, not what it sounds like in theory.

When the brief is similar to one of these, model the sentence structure. When the brief is new territory, look at the range and find the closest analogue.

Engagement = likes + replies + reposts on X. LinkedIn is ranked by reactions (LinkedIn hides impressions).

Note on X Articles: they are upranked by the algorithm. The impressions-to-likes ratio is far higher than regular tweets — often 200-350x. A tweet with 55 likes might get 2K impressions. An article with 55 likes gets 14K.
