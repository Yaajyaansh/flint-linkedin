# ICP — Flint (flint.com)

## 2026 update: ICP has sharpened to performance marketers

As of the 2026 repositioning (see `first-party.md` — "2026 repositioning" section), Flint's primary ICP is stated explicitly as **performance marketers** — specifically people who own and actively spend against a Google Ads account, not demand gen/growth marketers broadly. The profile below still holds directionally (same company size, stage, industry), but sharpen the framing: this person's day job is the ad account itself (bids, budgets, search terms, negative keywords, landing page assignment per ad group), and their core anxiety is CAC creeping up as they scale spend — not "we need more pages" in the abstract. The site's own CTA banner states this directly: "Scaling Spend Shouldn't Scale Your CAC."

## Primary ICP

**Who they are:**
Performance marketer / paid search lead — demand gen manager, growth marketer, or ABM lead — at a B2B SaaS / AI company, running meaningful spend on Google Ads. IC or small-team lead. Reports to VP Marketing or CMO.

**Company profile:**
- Size: 30–500 employees
- ARR: Series A–C ($2M–$50M ARR)
- Industry: AI-native, dev tools, B2B SaaS (especially sales tech, fintech, infrastructure)
- Stage: Growth — actively running paid acquisition, starting ABM motion, or scaling SEO
- Geography: US (SF Bay Area, NYC, remote-friendly)

**Day-to-day reality:**
They own campaign performance. They're the person who gets paged when Google Ads CPC spikes and conversion tanks. They're trying to spin up pages for a new campaign, a new vertical, or a named account list — and they're blocked on dev bandwidth or designer availability. They use Clay, HubSpot, and Claude; they know what MCP is.

**Goals:**
- Lower CPL, higher conversion rate on paid campaigns
- Stand up an ABM motion with personalized pages for 50–500 accounts
- Build out comparison and AEO pages before competitors own those searches
- Do more with a smaller team (often 1-3 people in marketing at their stage)

**Fears:**
- Ad spend going to a generic homepage with 0 conversions (it's happening right now)
- Being behind competitors on AEO — losing the ChatGPT/Perplexity citation game
- Their marketer leaving and the institutional knowledge of "how we build pages" walking out the door
- Looking like an AI laggard in a room full of AI-native companies

**Frustrations:**
- Dev queue backlog for landing pages (3-day wait for a 30-minute task)
- Generic AI outputs that don't match their brand
- Spending half a sprint briefing a contractor
- Running the same generic page across 5 ad groups and wondering why CVR is low

**Where they hang out:**
- Communities: Pavilion, Demand Gen Visionaries, Exit Five, Women in Revenue
- Events: SaaStr, HubSpot INBOUND, Pavilion Summit
- Newsletters: Exit Five, MKT1 Newsletter, The GTM Newsletter
- LinkedIn (actively) — they're the ones following Lenny, Elena Verna, MKT1

## Secondary ICP

**Who they are:**
Founder or CEO doing their own marketing at an early-stage startup. Pre-marketing-hire. Managing demand gen alongside product and sales.

**Company profile:**
- Size: 2–30 employees
- Stage: Pre-seed to Series A
- They are the marketing team

**Day-to-day reality:**
They wrote their own landing page copy, they're running their own Google Ads, and they're using Claude Code for basically everything. They want to ship pages faster without breaking design consistency. They may have found Flint through the Claude Code plugin or the MCP.

**Goals:**
- Get a comparison page live before a competitor owns the "vs" search
- Build ABM pages for their top 10 target accounts before the first outbound send
- Not have their website look unpolished relative to well-funded competitors

## Who is NOT a good fit

- Content-only teams with no paid acquisition or ABM play — the ROI case is weak
- Enterprise companies with heavy compliance/IT procurement cycles (unless they have a champion who can self-serve)
- Teams who want to own the codebase and won't let a platform manage their pages
- Consumer companies (Flint is explicitly B2B GTM focused)
- Teams running purely organic/inbound with no personalization need
