# Pricing — Flint (flint.com)

## Packages / Tiers

| Tier | Price | What's included | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0/month | 160 credits/month, one-time design system import, limited bandwidth hosting, API/MCP access | Exploring / testing; early-stage founders |
| Starter | $96/month (annual) | 12,000 credits/year, custom domain or subdomain, 10GB bandwidth, email support, no Flint badge | Small teams shipping 1-5 pages/month |
| Pro | $400/month (annual) | 60,000 credits/year, root domain hosting (yoursite.com/pages), 50GB bandwidth, Slack support | Growth teams running paid, ABM, and SEO at scale |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom credits, custom bandwidth, multiple websites, multi-time design system sync, password protection, SSO/SAML, RBAC, Growth Experts, dedicated CSM | Companies running campaigns at scale, enterprise security requirements |

**Note on credits:**
- A rich multi-section page from scratch: ~1,000 credits
- Duplicating an existing page + generating new content: ~200 credits
- Credits do not roll over (monthly or annual cycle)
- Annual billing = ~20% savings vs. monthly

## Typical deal size

Self-serve: $96–$400/month. Enterprise: custom (typically $1K+/month based on volume).

## Sales motion

[x] Self-serve (Free + Starter + Pro — no sales call needed)
[x] Sales-assisted (Enterprise — demo or discovery call via flint.com/contact-sales)

## How pricing comes up in content

Michelle references pricing lightly and directionally — "first two pages free", "free audit, no login", "try it at flint.com". She doesn't post pricing tables or make comp arguments in posts. The positioning is that Flint costs less than a single contractor landing page at Pro tier.

## Content guardrails around pricing

- Don't reference specific monthly dollar amounts in posts (let the website handle it)
- "Free" and "no login required" for the audit tool is a strong CTA — use it freely
- "14-day free trial" for Starter and Pro is the primary conversion play — can reference this
- Don't make pricing comparisons to competitors by name
- Enterprise pricing is custom — never suggest a number, direct to sales
