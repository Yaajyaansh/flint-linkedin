# Pricing — Flint (flint.com)

> Updated 2026-10. Pricing is now sales-assisted and based on a managed plan, not the earlier self-serve credit tiers. The old Free/Starter/Pro table is kept at the bottom as legacy. Check with the team before treating it as current.

## Current model

A flat platform fee plus a percentage of the media Flint works on. The percentage is lower on the Advised plan and higher on Fully managed. Exact figures are on flint.com/pricing and in sales conversations.

| Plan | What it is | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Enterprise 360 (fully managed)** | A Google Ads expert manages the account: account structure audits, weekly reports and strategy, biweekly meetings. The Flint agent covers the account 24/7. Forward-deployed engineers can wire downstream conversions (CRM, Shopify, attribution). | Companies that want to move fast and want an expert running the account |
| **Enterprise (advised)** | Flint proposes ads changes and builds the pages. The customer's team or agency reviews and approves what ships, with monthly guidance from a Client Services Manager. | Teams with someone to review the agent's actions |
| **Agencies** | The Advised plan, priced per client account: a flat platform fee per client plus 0.75% of the media Flint advises on. Agency terms start at five accounts and are set on the call. | Agencies running paid search for clients |

On every plan, nothing ships without a person signing off, and every change is logged and reversible.

## Sales motion

Sales-assisted. CTAs are "Book a demo" (B2B), "Talk to sales" (DTC), and "Talk to partnerships" (agencies). The entry point is a 2 to 3 week pilot, with a 30-minute setup.

## How pricing comes up in content

Michelle references pricing lightly and directionally, and never posts pricing tables. A pilot or demo is the next step, not a price.

## Content guardrails around pricing

- Don't state specific dollar amounts or percentages in posts. The website handles it. The 0.75% agency figure is for sales conversations.
- Don't claim a free tier, free trial, or "free audit, no login" CTA unless the team confirms it still exists under the new model.
- Don't make pricing comparisons to competitors by name.
- Never suggest a number for Enterprise. Direct to a demo.
