## Key messages by funnel stage

**Top of funnel (awareness):**
The era of building landing pages by hand is over. AI-native companies ship entire campaigns of pages in the time it used to take to brief a designer. The companies that figure this out first will move 10x faster than the ones that don't.

**Middle of funnel (consideration):**
Your highest-converting pages aren't the ones that exist — they're the ones you're missing. Industry pages, comparison pages, ABM pages, keyword-specific ad pages. Flint audits your site and tells you exactly what's costing you conversions, then builds it for you.

**Bottom of funnel (decision):**
Flint clones your design system and ships pages that look like your brand built them. You get the first two pages free. No engineer, no contractor, no design queue. Most teams are live in under an hour.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "AI pages look AI-generated / off-brand" | Flint's multi-agent design system cloning is what makes it different. Pages are indistinguishable from hand-built. This is the research-grade problem Flint solved. |
| "We already have a dev team / Webflow" | Flint works alongside your existing stack (integrates with Webflow, Framer, Cloudflare, etc.). Proves value inside existing setup, replaces the bottleneck over time. |
| "We can just vibe code with Claude" | A base model doesn't know your brand, your audience, or what converts. Flint does. Anyone can vibe code a page; productizing it at scale is the hard part. |
| "We don't have budget" | Free tier + free audit. First two pages free. Pro is $400/month — less than one contractor landing page. |
| "We don't run enough volume to need this" | Flint's audit will show you the pages you're missing — the ones costing you conversions you don't know you're losing. |

## Messages to avoid

- "AI-powered" used without specificity (everyone says this)
- "Seamless" / "easy" / "intuitive" — show don't tell
- Claiming to replace engineers (they integrate with eng, not replace)
- Vague customer references — always name the company and the result
- Anything that positions Flint as a template-based tool
