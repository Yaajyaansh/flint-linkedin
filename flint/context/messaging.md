# Messaging — Flint (flint.com)

> Updated 2026-10 for the managed Google Ads agent positioning (see `positioning.md`). Older pages-only proof points are kept below as established proof.

## Core message

Scaling spend shouldn't scale your CAC. Flint is a managed Google Ads agent that also builds the pages. It monitors your account 24/7, tests keywords, bids, and ad copy, and ships an intent-matched page for every ad group, with a Google Ads strategist on your account and a person signing off on every change.

## Key messages by funnel stage

**Top of funnel:** The weekly review produces a list that takes hours to execute, and those hours don't exist. Marketers already know what's wrong with the account. The problem was never insight, it was execution time.

**Middle of funnel:** Ads and pages belong in one loop. Anyone can generate a page, but nothing else tells you which page won, against what traffic, and what to build because of it.

**Bottom of funnel:** Connect the account and the first changes ship within days. A 2 to 3 week pilot tells you exactly what Flint is worth. A strategist is on your account, nothing ships without sign-off, and every change is logged and reversible.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Why not build this with Claude Code or Cursor?" | You can generate a page with any AI tool. You can't generate the feedback. Building a v1 you'd trust with your spend takes six months of multiple senior engineers plus an ads practitioner, then an engineer every day. DIY AI scripts have also gotten Google Ads accounts banned. |
| "Is it safe for my ad account?" | Flint works directly with Google. Every write path is reviewed and rate-limited. |
| "Will it blow my budget or change my bids?" | The agent tests keywords, bids, and ad copy. A strategist guides it, nothing ships without a person signing off, and every change is logged and reversible. |
| "Can I keep my Shopify theme or repo?" | Your site stays where it is. Pages live on your subdomain because the loop only runs fast when one system finds the opportunity, publishes the page, and measures it. |
| "Do you do PMax, Shopping, or Meta?" | Search today. Meta is in beta. PMax and Shopping aren't managed yet. |
| "Can I use it from Claude or Cursor?" | Yes for pages, through the MCP server. Ads changes and experiments aren't exposed through the MCP yet. |
| "How does pricing work?" | A flat platform fee plus a percentage of the media Flint works on. See `pricing.md`. |
| "AI pages look off-brand" | Pages pull from your design system (Figma import and an editable brand kit) and carry your brand voice. |

## Messages to avoid

- Calling Flint a page builder, a template tool, or a content tool.
- Saying Flint doesn't touch bids or budgets. It tests bids. Say what the safeguards are instead.
- "AI-powered" without specificity, and "seamless" or "easy". Show the mechanism.
- Claiming to replace engineers, ad buyers, or agencies. Flint changes what they spend time on.
- Promising PMax, Shopping, or Meta results. Meta is in beta.
- Vague customer references. Name the company and the result, with the right measure.
