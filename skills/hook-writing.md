# Hook Writing Skill

A hook is the first 1-3 lines of a LinkedIn post. It is the only thing that determines whether someone stops scrolling. Write it last, after you know the full post.

---

## What makes a hook work

A hook works when it creates **genuine incompleteness** — the reader physically cannot stop reading because they don't yet have the resolution their brain is searching for.

It fails when:
- It gives away the insight upfront
- It asks a rhetorical question (see forbidden structures)
- It uses round numbers (use 47, not 50; use $14,200, not $15k)
- It sounds like a hook — formulaic, symmetrical, AI-patterned
- It opens with "Most people..." or "The truth is..."

---

## The 12 hook patterns (with real examples)

### 1. Milestone + Tension
State a real achievement, then immediately introduce a contradiction or cost.

> I just signed our first 4 clients and booked $14,000 MRR for Profit Labs. But two weeks ago, I almost shut the whole business down. Here's what changed:

> By year 3, we hit $4,200,000 in EBITDA at Matter Made with 67% profit margins. But my close mentor told me I was "too profitable" and actually choking the business. Turns out he was right.

> We lost 2 of our longest-standing clients in July after 3 years together. Then, we signed our 3 largest deals ever and got back on track for 40% YoY growth. Here's the recap:

**Template:** `[Achieved X] for [company]. But [short time period] ago, I almost [gave up / shut it down]. Here's what changed:`

---

### 2. Then vs. Now (Time Contrast)
Two data points separated by time. No editorializing — just the numbers, let the gap do the work.

> 4 years ago, we charged $45,000/mo at Matter Made. Today, our retainer fee starts at $8,000/mo. It flies in the face of everything I've ever said about "raising your prices," but it was the single best decision we could've made.

> 5 years ago, my co-founder and I would walk past a 800 sqft office everyday that seemed like a complete pipedream. Today, we have 25+ employees in a space 5x as nice.

> 4 years ago: $0 in funding, 0 customers, small team. Today: $14M in funding, 500 lawyers using our product, 20+ team members. In between:

> End of 2023: freelancer always searching for projects. End of 2024: CEO of 8-person agency with a full pipeline.

**Template:** `[Time period] ago, [specific situation]. Today, [specific contrasting situation].`

---

### 3. Uncomfortable Admission
Confess something counterintuitive, embarrassing, or costly. Specificity = credibility.

> I'm sick of seeing people call their $1.2M/year revenue agency a "7-figure business". Here's what a 7-figure business actually looks like:

> My goal for 2025 is to have a less profitable year. Let me explain.

> I gave up 20% equity in my agency and it was the best decision I've ever made.

> I lost $25k last month...

**Template:** `I [did counterintuitive thing / admitted uncomfortable truth]. [One-line consequence or resolution signal]:`

---

### 4. Specific Output Claim
Lead with an ultra-specific number. No round numbers. Let the claim carry the weight.

> 144 LinkedIn posts. $5,100 in ad spend. 138 booked meetings. Here's the step-by-step workflow most agencies will never show you.

> I wasted $150k testing 100+ AI sales tools so you don't have to. Here's the exact 25-tool stack that took us to $6M ARR.

> I've generated ~40M social impressions in 2024. Here are 5 rules every single one of my videos follow.

> I've audited 200+ founder profiles this year. 73% made the same fatal mistake.

**Template:** `[Specific number] [specific output]. [Specific number] [specific outcome]. Here's [what I'll share]:`

---

### 5. Hard Prohibition
Tell someone what NOT to do. More memorable than telling them what to do.

> Don't spend (any more) money on marketing until everyone on your marketing team knows:

> Your CEO writes a banger thought leadership post and it gets 100s of engagements from your ICP... DON'T send your SDRs or AEs or anyone else who's not the CEO itself to reach out to those who engaged.

> If you want to run a solid influencer marketing campaign, DON'T make the same mistake Febreze made partnering with Khloe Kardashian. Here's what happened:

**Template:** `Don't [common thing] until [prerequisite condition]:`
