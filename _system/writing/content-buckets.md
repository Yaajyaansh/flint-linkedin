# Content Buckets — Funnel Strategy

## How funnels work on LinkedIn

Top of funnel → get eyeballs from your ICP and adjacent audiences
Middle of funnel → nurture those eyeballs toward the problem you solve
Bottom of funnel → convert the nurtured audience into leads / customers

Aim roughly: 40% top / 40% middle / 20% bottom per month.

---

## Two ways to use this file

**Path A — I have context (voice note, story, article, thought)**
→ Skip the prompts. Go straight to Hook Writer with your raw input.
→ Idea Scout extracts the angle, picks the funnel stage, selects the hook method.

**Path B — I have nothing**
→ Idea Scout picks prompts from the 214 below, matches them to the client's buckets, generates 5-10 ideas.
→ Human picks one. Hook Writer writes 3 hooks. Human picks one. Writer drafts.

6. Highlight a recent update to your product. Share how it came together, and why it makes your customers' experience better.
7. Imagine a prospect is very low in your funnel. Use this post to dispel any risk they may feel exists with choosing your product.
