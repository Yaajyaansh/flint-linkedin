# Forbidden Sentence Structures

The Checker agent flags any of these. Writer never uses them.

---

## 1. Reversal Framing / False Negatives

NEVER use:
- "It's not about X, it's about Y"
- "It's not X, it's Y"
- "This isn't A. It's B."
- "X isn't the problem. Y is."
- "Customer experience is not a cost center. It's a conversion tool."
- "Most people think X. The truth is Y."
- "Everyone focuses on A. The real leverage is B."

---

## 2. Rhetorical Questions

NEVER ask or answer rhetorical questions:
- "The reality?"
- "And honestly?"
- "The truth?"
- "Why does this matter?"
- "What does this mean?"
- "Want to know how?"
- Any question not expecting a literal answer

---

## 3. Anaphora (Parallel Repetition)

NEVER repeat structural openings across sentences:
- "More X. More Y. More Z."
- "Better A. Better B. Better C."
- "Higher X. Higher Y. Higher Z."
- "Your X. Your Y. Your Z."
- "You're A. You're B. You're C."

Example to avoid: "Higher branded search volume. Higher direct traffic. Higher returning customer revenue."

---

## 4. Asyndeton (Staccato Lists Without Conjunctions)

NEVER stack fragmented sentences:
- "Strategy. Taste. Judgment. Final decisions."
- Period-separated nouns
- Sentence fragments stacked vertically
- Fragment + punchy conclusion: "Unsexy problems. But yours to fix."
- Stacked sentences starting with "Your" or "You're"

Example to avoid: "Your creative doesn't stop mid-scroll. Your offer doesn't make anyone feel stupid. You're not sending enough emails."

---

## 5. Sentences Starting With "Most"

NEVER:
- "Most people do X"
- "Most founders think Y"
- "Most brands are drowning in data..."

---

## 6. Reversal With Common Belief Setup

NEVER set up a common belief just to knock it down:
- "Most people think X. But [contrarian take]."
- "Everyone focuses on [thing]. The real leverage is [opposite]."
- "The truth is..." (as reversal)
- "The reality..." (as reversal)

---

## 7. Mechanism Contrast With Abstract Superiority

Avoid:
- "The math compounds in ways that acquisition can't."

State concrete effects instead.

---

## Hook-Specific Failures

Hooks FAIL if they:
- Use rhetorical questions
- Use reversal framing
- Give away the core insight in the opening line
- Use formulaic structures ("X reasons...", "Here's what I learned...")
- Lack genuine incompleteness
- Use round numbers instead of ultra-specific figures (50 vs 47, 100 vs 103)
- Start with "Most people..." or "The truth is..." or "The reality..."
- Have AI symmetry or dramatic over-formatting
- Are too vague or generic
