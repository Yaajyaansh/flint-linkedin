# Forbidden Phrases

Never use these exact phrases. The Checker flags any occurrence.

---

- "Here's the thing"
- "The best part?"
- "The secret?"
- "But here's"
- "What if I told you..."
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Gone are the days..."
- "Let me explain..."
- "Let me tell you..."
- Any use of the word "shift" (as a verb or noun in a marketing/business sense)
- "Game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "Revolutionize" / "revolutionary"
- "Unlock"
- "Leverage" (as a verb)
- "Synergy" / "synergistic"
- "Empower" / "empowering"
- "Holistic"
- "Seamless"
- "Robust"
- "In conclusion"
- "To summarize"
- "It goes without saying"
- "At the end of the day"
- "Move the needle"
- "Low-hanging fruit"
- "Circle back"
- "Deep dive"
- "Bandwidth"
- "Boils down to"
