# Forbidden Writing Patterns

Patterns the Writer avoids. The Checker scans for these.

---

## Cliché Openings
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Gone are the days..."
- Any corporate buzzword openings
- "As we navigate..."
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of..."

---

## Rhetorical Flourishes
- "The best part?"
- "The secret?"
- "What if I told you..."
- Staccato phrases: "No fluff. No filler. Just results."
- Any sentence that exists only to create drama without adding information

---

## Generic AI Patterns
- Unnecessary adverbs: "incredibly," "absolutely," "truly," "deeply"
- Filler affirmations: "Great question," "Absolutely," "Certainly"
- Artificial symmetry in sentence structure (every sentence same length/rhythm)
- Dramatic cadence that sounds performative
- Corporate buzzwords or jargon
- Over-explaining context the reader already knows
- Listing things in threes for aesthetic reasons rather than content reasons
- Starting multiple consecutive sentences with the same word

---

## Abstract Subjects Acting Like People
- Don't make abstract nouns the active subject of a sentence
- Avoid lines like:
  - "Clarity drives execution."
  - "Good leadership is deliberate."
  - "Accountability builds trust."
- Recast so people are the subject and the abstract noun is a modifier, object, or prepositional phrase
- Prefer:
  - "Teams execute better when they're clear on the plan."
  - "CEOs who lead deliberately get more out of their teams."
  - "Managers build trust by owning their mistakes."

---

## Structural Patterns That Signal AI
- Ending every section with a motivational one-liner
- Posts that follow: problem → insight → solution → CTA with perfect symmetry
- Using "Moreover," "Furthermore," "Additionally" as connectors
- Transitioning with "Now, let's talk about..."
- Starting the CTA with "If you found this valuable..."
- "I hope this helps" or any variation

---

## Format Anti-Patterns
- Using bullet points for everything — mix formats
- Making every line a separate paragraph
- Emoji as decoration (only use if genuinely part of the client's voice)
- Overusing bold — bold is for structure, not emphasis on random words
- Headers in a post that should flow as prose
